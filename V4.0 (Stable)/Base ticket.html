Important Information

  • Check-in Time: Please arrive at the airport at least 4 hours before departure. Check-in counters generally close 120 minutes before the scheduled departure. It is advisable to confirm with your airline.
  • Transit Visa Requirement: Travelers are responsible for ensuring they are eligible to enter the destination or transit countries. Shree Dhanlaxmi Travel accepts no liability regarding eligibility. Always check regulatory websites before travel.
  • Travel Documentation: Passengers must carry a valid passport and visa for international flights. The passport should be valid for at least six months from the day of departure. For domestic flights, any government-issued photo ID, such as an Aadhar card, driving license, or voter ID, is acceptable. Please present hard copies of your visa or government ID at immigration or check-in as required.
  • Baggage Policies: Adhere to the airline’s baggage dimension guidelines to avoid additional charges or denied boarding. Refer to the airline's website for detailed information.
  • Travel Insurance: We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance to cover medical expenses, lost luggage, and flight cancellations. Secure your peace of mind by ensuring coverage for all aspects of your trip.
  • Travel Precautions: Always verify the latest travel rules and eligibility on the regulatory website of the country you are traveling to. Changes in guidelines may occur without notice.
